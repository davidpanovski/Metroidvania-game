# Metroidvania Knight Game

A 2D action-platformer inspired by **Hollow Knight**, built with **Unity and C#**. Explore caves as a knight, fight enemies with melee attacks and spells, and navigate platforms and hazards using jumps and dashes.

## About the Project

This is a personal side project that I work on in my spare time. It gives me a space to experiment with game development and build a Metroidvania-style experience one feature at a time.

Hollow Knight is a major inspiration, especially for the movement, directional combat, mana-based healing, and bench checkpoints. The project is still a work in progress, with room to expand and improve its gameplay and world.

## Gameplay and Features

- **Exploration and platforming:** Travel through cave areas, cross platforms, and avoid hazards such as spikes.
- **Movement:** Jump and dash to navigate the environment and reposition during fights.
- **Directional melee combat:** Attack in front of you, above you, or below you while airborne.
- **Spells and healing:** Spend mana to cast fireballs or hold the healing button to recover health. Managing mana means balancing offense and recovery.
- **Enemy encounters:** Face different enemy types, including crawlers, chargers, and bats.
- **Bench checkpoints:** Rest at benches to recover health and mana, save your progress, and set a respawn point.
- **Death and recovery:** Death leaves behind a shade and reduces your available mana capacity until it is restored.

## How to Play

Explore the caves, learn enemy attack patterns, and use movement and directional attacks to get through encounters. Keep an eye on your health and mana: both healing and fireballs use the same resource.

Visit benches as you progress. Press **V** when nearby to rest and save before continuing your journey.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D | Move left / right |
| W | Jump |
| C | Dash |
| Left mouse button | Melee attack |
| W / S + attack | Attack upward / downward (downward while airborne) |
| F | Cast a fireball using mana |
| Hold right mouse button | Heal using mana |
| V near a bench | Rest and save |

## Development Status

This is an ongoing hobby project developed on the side. Features, levels, and balancing may change as development continues.
